# Your mission: the public pitch 🎤

3 minutes. Audience: **non-technical** — imagine pitching a city council for funding.

1. **Hook** — the human story: who needs this and why
2. **Solution** — your robot, ZERO jargon
3. **Evidence** — what your testing and iteration showed
4. **Ask** — what you'd need to build the real thing

---

# Kill the jargon ⚔️

| Don't say | Say |
|---|---|
| "actuator" | "the motor that moves the arm" |
| "failure-mode analysis" | "we figured out what breaks first" |
| "iterate" | "we tested it and made it better" |

Mentors will flag jargon during rehearsal. **If your grandmother wouldn't get it, rewrite it.**

---

# Rehearsal rules 🎬

- **Two full run-throughs**, timed
- Every team member has a speaking role
- Feedback round after each: clarity, pacing, eye contact
- Nervous is normal — preparation beats nerves every time

---

# Showtime 🌟

Present to the audience. Then: **Q&A**.

Handling questions like a pro:
- Pause. Breathe. It's okay to think.
- Don't know? *"Great question — we'd need to test that."*
- Disagreement? *"That's a fair point — here's our reasoning..."*
