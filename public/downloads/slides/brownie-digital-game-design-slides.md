---

# What makes a game a GAME? 🤔

Let's take apart a game you already know — tag, or tic-tac-toe.

Every game has:
- A **goal** 🎯 — what you're trying to do
- **Rules** 📜 — what you're allowed to do
- Something that makes it **fair and fun** 😄

**Every video game designer starts with these same three pieces.**

---

# Designers plan FIRST 📝

Before we touch a screen, fill in your **game recipe**:

1. My game's **goal** is…
2. **How you win**…
3. What makes it **fun**…

Keep it **small**: one goal, one way to win.

---

# Build: your Collector Game 🌟

Follow your mentor, one step at a time:

1. A **player sprite** that moves with the arrow keys
2. A **good thing** to collect — a star, an apple 🍎
3. A **score** that goes up when you collect it
4. A **win message** when your score hits the target

Finished early? Add a sound 🔊 or a "bad thing" to avoid!

---

# Bugs are GOOD! 🐛

When your game doesn't work, that's a **bug**.

Finding one means you're doing **real programming**.

Fixing it is called **debugging**.

---

# Playtest Swap 🔄

Swap seats. Play your neighbor's game!

Then give **two stars and a wish**:
- ⭐ One kind thing you liked
- ⭐ One more kind thing you liked
- 💫 One idea to make it even better

**Playtesting is a real game-design job.**
