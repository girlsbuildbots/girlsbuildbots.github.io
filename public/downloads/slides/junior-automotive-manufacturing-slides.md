# Automotive Manufacturing 🏭
## Junior Automotive Engineering Badge — Girls Build Bots
### Presented by your robotics mentors!

---

# The scale problem 📈

One great prototype isn't a car company.

Factories make **thousands** — identical, and safe.

### How?

---

# The Assembly Line 🔗

Each worker **masters one step** as the product flows past.

Today the troop becomes a factory —
and we'll **measure it** like one.

---

# Round 1: Craft Production 🧍

Build one complete car **alone**. Every step. **8 minutes.**

Then we tally:
- How many finished?
- Line them up — how **identical** are they?

---

# The honest results 📊

Slow. And every car is a little different.

### That's craft production.

---

# Round 2: The Assembly Line 🏭

One Scout (or pair) per step, plus a **Quality Control inspector**
with the spec card.

**10 minutes. Go!**

QC rejects anything missing a part — it goes back to the
station that missed it.

---

# Defects are information 📋

**Not shame.**

QC rejects **kindly and specifically**:
> "Missing left headlight."

Never "you did it wrong."

### Would you ride in a car nobody inspected?

---

# Read the board 📊

| Round | Made | Passed QC | Defects |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo | | | |
| Line | | | |

More cars. More consistent. **And one station drowning in a pile.**

### That's a bottleneck. 🍾

---

# Kaizen Round: improve the line 🔧

Real factories **never run the same line twice**.
That's **continuous improvement**.

Team huddle:
- Where was the **bottleneck**?
- What fixes it? Move a person? Split a step? Pre-stage supplies?

**Apply your best ideas. Run 10 more minutes.**

---

# Compare all three rounds 🏆

**Solo → Line → Improved Line**

The improvement round almost always wins big.

### And YOU engineered that gain.

---

# Robot moment 🤖

Build season runs like this:
- **Parallel stations** — wiring, programming, machining
- **Inspections** before competition
- Constant **process improvements** between events

---

# Debrief 🎯

1. Why was the line faster?
2. Why did QC matter?
3. What did **your** improvement change?

**Count the total fleet — manufacturing is a team sport.** 🎉

---

# What you accomplished ✅

- Explained why factories use assembly lines instead of solo builds
- Worked a station and saw how quality control catches defects
- Identified a bottleneck from real production data
- Proposed an improvement, tested it, and compared the rounds
