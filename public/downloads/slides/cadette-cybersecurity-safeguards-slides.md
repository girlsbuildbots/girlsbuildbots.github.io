# Cybersecurity Safeguards 🛡️
## Cadette Cybersecurity Badge — Girls Build Bots
### Presented by your robotics mentors

---

# Attackers don't hack computers

### They hack **people**.

Social engineering. Urgency. Impersonation.

The defense: a calm, skeptical mind — plus a few strong technical habits.

---

# Rule, stated out loud 📢

### Every credential today is **invented**.

We will not log into anything during this session.

**The rule is the lesson.**

---

# Authentication: how does a system know you're you? 🔑

| Factor | Example |
|---|---|
| Something you **know** | a passphrase |
| Something you **have** | a code on your phone — **2FA** |
| Something you **are** | a fingerprint |

*Passphrase refresher: length beats cleverness. Three random words.*

---

# Two locks beat one 🔒🔒

### A stolen passphrase, alone, fails against 2FA.

And the grown-up tool: a **password manager** —
one strong master passphrase, unique passwords everywhere else.

**Deliverable:** your written personal authentication plan.

---

# Social Engineering Defense 🎭

Work the scenario deck. For each message, name the
**manipulation lever**:

| Lever | Sounds like |
|---|---|
| **Urgency** | "in the next 10 minutes!" |
| **Authority** | "this is the school office" |
| **Emotion** | "your friend is stranded" |
| **Reward** | "you've been selected" |

---

# The counter-move ✋

1. **Slow down.**
2. **Verify through a separate channel** — call the real number,
   ask the real friend in person
3. **Never act on pressure.**

---

# One message in the deck is REAL 🎯

Because the skill is **discrimination**, not blanket paranoia.

Which team sorts the full deck correctly *and* names every lever?

---

# Privacy Audit Workshop 🔍

From memory — **no logging in**:

- What accounts exist?
- What does each one **know** about you?
- **Who can see what?**
- What would you change?

*Think of it as taking inventory of your own house keys.*

---

# Your top three 📋

Three concrete actions to complete at home **with a parent**:

- Tighten one setting
- Enable **2FA** somewhere
- Retire an unused account

---

# Robot moment 🤖

We protect team accounts and code with unique passphrases and 2FA.

And "someone urgently needs the team login" is a message
you **verify** — never obey.

---

# Defense Drill ⚡

Rapid fire. We read a scenario; you call the **lever**
and the **counter-move**.

**Closing, honestly:** which lever would most likely have
worked on you *before* today?

### Self-awareness is the strongest safeguard in the room.

---

# What you accomplished ✅

- Identified the manipulation lever in a social-engineering attempt
- Applied the verify-through-another-channel counter-move

# Level up 🚀

- Teach the four levers to someone who'd fall for one
- You now see the levers — they don't work as well on someone who sees them
