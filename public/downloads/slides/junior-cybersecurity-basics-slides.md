# How does information actually travel? 🌐

Today's layer is the **how-it-works** layer:

- Networks 🕸️
- Messages hopping between computers 💬
- Why data is **valuable** 💎

---

# The Human Internet 📨

We're building a network — out of you.

1. **Senders** write a short message on a card
2. Seal it in an envelope, addressed across the room
3. It travels **hand to hand through "routers"** to get there

*Routers = the Scouts in the middle.*

---

# Round 2: the snoop 👀

One router is a **snoop**. Unsealed cards get peeked at.

**Debrief:** on the internet, messages hop through many computers.
Anyone in the path could look.

### Which is why some messages need protecting.

---

# Secret Codes 🔑

The solution to the snoop: write your message in a **cipher**
before you send it.

1. Encode with the substitution sheet
2. Send it
3. Your partner decodes it

**The snoop gets a coded card — and can't read it.**

---

# That's encryption 🛡️

A cipher is the core idea of **encryption**.

### Encryption is the internet's envelope.

---

# What's your data worth? 💎

Sort the cards. Which would a stranger want most — and why?

**password** > **address** > **school** > **favorite song**

---

# Your digital footprint 👣

The trail of information you leave online **adds up**.

Here's the honest version:

### Data is valuable, so people try to collect it.

That's exactly why we **choose** what to share.

# Robot moment 🤖

Our robot and its **driver station** talk over a network during matches.

The field's network is locked down so **no one else** can send
our robot commands.
