# Automotive Manufacturing 🏭
## Brownie Automotive Engineering Badge — Girls Build Bots
### Presented by your robotics mentors!

---

# One car… or a HUNDRED? 🚗🚗🚗

You designed a car. You engineered a car.

### Now: how do you make **hundreds** — the same, fast, and well made?

---

# The Assembly Line 🔗

Instead of **one person building a whole car**…

Each person does **one job** as the car moves past.

**Henry Ford** made this famous.

---

# Round 1: Everyone Solo 🧍

Make one complete paper car **all by yourself**.
Every single step.

⏱️ We're timing you!

---

# What did you notice? 🤔

- Was it **slow**? 🐢
- Did everyone's car come out a little **different**?

Now let's try it the factory way…

---

# Round 2: Build the LINE! 🏭

Each Scout takes ONE station:

1. **Fold** 📄
2. **Wheels** 🛞
3. **Windows** 🪟
4. **Decorate** 🎨
5. **Inspect** 🔍

The cars move down the line. You do only your job — over and over!

---

# The Quality Inspector 🔍

One Scout has the **checklist**.

Any car missing a part? **Send it back!**

**Quality matters as much as speed.**

---

# Compare the rounds ⏱️

- Was the line **faster**?
- Were the cars more **alike**?
- What happened when one station got **backed up**?

---

# The factory truth 🏭

The line made **more cars, more alike, faster**…

…but **everyone had to work as a team**.

And **one slow station slowed everybody down.**

That's real manufacturing!

---

# Robot moment 🤖

Our team splits build season into roles and stations —
**programming, building, wiring** — so lots of work happens at once.

Just like a factory line!

---

# You did it! 🎉

Today you:
- ✅ Explained what an assembly line is and why factories use one
- ✅ Worked a station as part of a manufacturing team
- ✅ Compared building solo vs. building on a line
- ✅ Said why quality checking matters

**Let's count our cars — that's the TEAM's total!** 🎊
