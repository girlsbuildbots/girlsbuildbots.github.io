# App Development 📱
## Brownie Coding for Good Badge — Girls Build Bots
### Presented by your robotics mentors!

---

# We're not just coding today 💚

We're **solving a problem for a real person**.

That's what "coding for good" means.

---

# Apps are problem-solvers 🔧

Think about apps you know. For each one ask:

### **Whose problem does this solve?**

- A weather app → helps you know what to wear ☔
- A maps app → helps you not get lost 🗺️
- A timer → helps you remember 🍳

Apps aren't magic. **Someone noticed a problem and built a fix.**

---

# Find a problem worth solving 🔍

What's a problem in your **school**, **troop**, **home**, or
**neighborhood**?

- Remembering to water the class plants 🌱
- Finding lost library books 📚
- Knowing when the bus comes 🚌

**No idea is too small.** Pick ONE problem your app will solve.

---

# Design your app on paper ✏️

This is how **real app designers** start!

**Screen 1** — Welcome: your app's name + what it does
**Screen 2** — Main screen: the buttons that solve the problem
**Screen 3** — What happens when you tap the main button

Paper apps don't need to work. They need to show the **idea**.

---

# Mentor question 💭

As you design, we'll keep asking you one thing:

### "What happens when someone taps **here**?"

---

# The Click Test 👆

With a partner:

1. One of you **uses** the paper app — point at the buttons
2. The designer **swaps the screens** by hand
3. Does it make sense? Fix it!

Real designers call this a **click-through test**.

---

# Robot moment 🤖

Before our team builds the robot's control app, we decide
**what it needs to do** and **who's going to use it**.

Same thinking as yours: *who needs this, and what does it do?*

---

# Share your app ✨

In one sentence:

### "My app is called ___ and it helps ___."
