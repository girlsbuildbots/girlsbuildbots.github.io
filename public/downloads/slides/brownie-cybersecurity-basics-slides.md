# Cybersecurity Basics 🔐
## Brownie Cybersecurity Badge — Girls Build Bots
### Presented by your robotics mentors!

---

# Today we become internet-smart 🧠

Not scared of the internet — **smart** about it.

Today: how information **travels**, and which information we **keep private**.

---

# Pass the Message 💬

Everybody in a circle!

1. We'll pass a message hand to hand: *"the robot is blue"*
2. That's a **network** — how information travels between computers
3. Now… we're adding a **listener** in the middle 👂

---

# What did we learn? 🤔

Messages travel through **many hands**.

Anyone along the way might hear.

**So some information we keep private.**

---

# Private, or OK to share? 🗂️

Let's sort these cards into two piles!

**OK to share** 😀
- Favorite animal
- Your team name

**Private** 🔒
- Full name + address
- Phone number
- Passwords

---

# Play Interland! 🎮

Time to play — one realm, together.

While you play, keep thinking:
**would I share this? Why or why not?**

---

# My rules ✋

Name **one thing** you'll keep private.

Name **one thing** that's fine to share.

---

# Robot moment 🤖

Our team keeps our robot code and passwords **private**,
so no other team can change our robot.

Being careful with information matters in robotics too!

---

# You did it! 🎉

Today you:
- ✅ Learned that a network is how information travels
- ✅ Sorted private information from share-able information
- ✅ Played an online-safety game and said what it taught you
- ✅ Named personal information you'll keep private

**Thumbs up if you can spot personal information now!** 👍
