# Automotive Engineering 🔧
## Brownie Automotive Engineering Badge — Girls Build Bots
### Presented by your robotics mentors!

---

# Today we make it MOVE 🏁

You designed a car. Now you'll **engineer** one that really rolls —
then make it **better**.

---

# What makes a car go? ⛰️

No engine today. We use a **ramp** and **gravity**!

- **Force** = a push or a pull 💪
- **Friction** = grippy slows you down 🐌, slippery lets you roll 💨

---

# Predict first! 🔮

### Will a **heavier** car or a **lighter** car roll farther?

Everybody guess!

**Engineers always predict before they test.**

---

# Build your roller 🛠️

Make a cardboard car with:
- **Axles** that go straight across
- **Wheels** that spin freely

**Goal:** it rolls straight down the ramp.

Wobbly wheels? That's a **real engineering problem** — let's solve it together!

---

# Test. Change. Test again. 🔁

This is the heart of engineering:

1. Roll your car down the ramp 🏁
2. **Measure** how far it went 📏
3. Write it down 📝
4. Change **ONE** thing
5. Test again — did it help?

---

# The rule: ONE thing at a time ☝️

Change **only one thing** between tests.

Change five things and you won't know **which one worked**.

That's called a **fair test**.

---

# What could you change? 🤔

- Add weight ⚖️
- Swap the wheels 🛞
- Straighten the axle 📏

Pick ONE. Predict. Test. Record.

---

# Robot moment 🤖

Our team tests the robot over and over,
changing **one thing at a time**, to make it work better.

Same fair-test habit as yours!

---

# Best IMPROVEMENT wins 🏆

Not "whose car is fastest."

### "Who made the **biggest improvement**?"

Share one change you made and what happened.

---

# You did it! 🎉

Today you:
- ✅ Learned that a force like gravity makes a car move
- ✅ Built a car with wheels that roll
- ✅ Measured how your car did
- ✅ Changed one thing, tested again, and knew if it improved

**You are an engineer now!**

---
