# Automotive Design 🚗
## Junior Automotive Engineering Badge — Girls Build Bots
### Presented by your robotics mentors!

---

# From idea to road 🛣️

The Automotive series follows the real industry pipeline:

**Design** it → **engineer** it → **manufacture** it

Today: design it for a **user** and a **purpose**.

---

# Every design is a trade-off ⚖️

```
              STYLE
               /\
              /  \
             /    \
      FUNCTION —— SAFETY
```

You **can't max out all three**. Designers choose deliberately.

---

# Which corner did each one pick? 🤔

| Vehicle | Favors | Gives up |
|---|---|---|
| Sports car | style + speed | cargo space |
| Minivan | function | style |
| Delivery truck | function | style |

### Designers don't pick everything — they choose for their user.

---

# The Design Brief 📋

Your team draws a brief card. Unpack it like professionals:

- **Who** is the user?
- What must it **do**? *(function)*
- What would hurt them if it failed? *(safety)*
- What would make them **love** it? *(style)*

---

# Write your requirements 📝

### Three **must-haves** + one **nice-to-have**

That's a real requirements list — with constraints.

---

# Sketch TWO. Choose ONE. ✍️

Sketch **two different concepts** — side view + top view, features labeled.

> Committing to your first idea is the rookie mistake.

Comparing two forces real design thinking.

**Then pick one — and say what it trades away.**

---

# Build the concept model 🛠️

Cardboard and foam. Show **every must-have feature**:

- A ramp that folds
- A market stall that opens
- Weatherproof mail access

It doesn't need to drive. It needs to **communicate the design**.

---

# Design Review 🎤

Each team presents:
1. Your **user**
2. Your **must-haves**
3. The **trade-off** you chose
4. The feature you're **proudest** of

Audience: one "what about…?" question per team.

**Defending your choices is the real job.**

---

# Robot moment 🤖

Our team designs the robot under the same triangle every season —
**speed vs. capability vs. not-breaking**.

Ask us what this year's robot traded away.

---

# What you accomplished ✅

- Explained how style, function, and safety trade off
- Turned a user's needs into a must-have requirements list
- Compared two concept sketches and justified your choice
- Built and presented a concept model of your design
