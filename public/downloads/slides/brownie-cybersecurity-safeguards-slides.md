# Cybersecurity Safeguards 🛡️
## Brownie Cybersecurity Badge — Girls Build Bots
### Presented by your robotics mentors!

---

# Today we PROTECT our information 🔒

Last time: how information travels.

Today: the **safeguards** that keep it safe.

---

# A password is like a latch 🔐

Look at this treasure box!

- A **weak** latch → anyone can open it 😟
- A **strong** latch → your things stay safe 😊

**A password is the latch on your information.**

---

# Strong or weak? 💪

Let's sort these **make-believe** password cards!

**Weak** 👎
- `1234`
- `password`

**Strong** 👍
- Long
- Mixed up
- Silly — but you can remember it!

### We NEVER share real passwords — not even with us!

---

# Play Interland: Tower of Treasure 🏰

Build strong "keys" through the game.

Remember the treasure box:
**long and unexpected is strong.**

---

# Ask a Grown-Up 🙋

For each card, vote: **safe**, or **stop and ask**?

- A pop-up says "YOU WON A PRIZE!" 🎁
- A game asks for your full name 📝
- Someone you don't know sends a message 📨

---

# Asking is a SUPERPOWER 🦸

Stopping to ask a trusted grown-up is not "being a baby."

It's the **smart, strong** choice.

Real cybersecurity experts ask each other for help every day.

---

# Robot moment 🤖

Our team uses **strong passwords** to protect our robot code
and our accounts.

Same safeguards — just for a robot!
