# Games are built *for players* 💚

A good designer asks:
- **Who** plays this?
- Is it **fair**?
- Is it **fun for everyone**?

That's **positive gaming** — the "for good" of this badge.

---

# Dissect a game like a designer 🔍

Pick a game everyone knows. Break it into its designer parts:

| Part | The question |
|---|---|
| **Goal** | what are you trying to do? |
| **Rules** | what are you allowed to do? |
| **Win condition** | how does it end? |
| **Difficulty curve** | does it get harder as you go? |

---

# Write the design doc 📄

**Every real studio starts here.** One page:

1. **Goal**
2. **Rules**
3. **Win condition**
4. What makes it **fun**
5. **Who it's for**

Scope rule: one goal, one obstacle, one way to win.

---

# No screens until the doc is done ✋

It's the difference between **designing** and **doodling**.

---

# Build YOUR game 💻

In MakeCode Arcade — building *your* design doc, not a clone:

1. Player sprite with arrow-key movement
2. Collectible + a **score counter**
3. **Win condition** — hit the target score → "YOU WIN"
4. **Challenge element** — an enemy that chases, or a countdown timer
5. Stretch: a second level, or increasing speed — that's your **difficulty curve**

---

# Structured Playtest 🔄

Swap seats. Play for **two minutes**. Leave two sticky notes:

- ⭐ **One star** — what's fun
- 💡 **One wish** — one change that would make it better

Always both. Feedback is a gift.

---

# Now iterate 🔧

Make **ONE improvement** based on your feedback.

### Iteration is the most important habit in game design.
