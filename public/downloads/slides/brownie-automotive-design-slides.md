# Automotive Design 🚗
## Brownie Automotive Engineering Badge — Girls Build Bots
### Presented by your robotics mentors!

---

# Every car starts with a QUESTION 💭

### "Who is this car **for**?"

Today you design a car for a real driver.

---

# Cars have a job and a driver 🧑‍🚒

Look at these vehicles. Who drives it? What must it do?

- 🚒 Fire truck → needs a big ladder
- 🍦 Ice-cream truck → needs a freezer
- 🏎️ Race car → needs to go FAST
- 🚐 Minivan → needs lots of seats

**Cars are designed around their driver's needs.**

---

# Meet your customer 🎴

Draw a **customer card**. This is who your car is for!

- A farmer 🌾
- A dog-walker 🐕
- A pizza deliverer 🍕
- A family with three kids 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

---

# Fill in your design sheet ✏️

1. **My driver** is…
2. **What they need** is…
3. **My car's special feature** is…

Designing for a **real customer** — not just "a cool car" — is the whole badge.

---

# Sketch FIRST, then build ✍️

Designers always draw before they build.

1. **Sketch** your car on paper
2. Then build a cardboard **concept model**
3. Make the wheels **turn**!

It doesn't have to drive. It has to **show your idea**.

---

# The Design Show 🎤

Show us your car in ONE sentence:

### "This is for a ___, so it has ___!"

*Example: "This is for a dog-walker, so it has a comfy pet seat!"*

---

# Gallery walk 🖼️

Walk around. Look at everyone's cars.

Give **one kind comment** to a designer.

---

# Robot moment 🤖

Our team designs our robot around the **year's challenge**.

We decide what it needs to **do** before we decide what it
**looks like** — just like designing a car for a driver.

---

# You did it! 🎉

Today you:
- ✅ Learned that vehicles are designed around a driver's needs
- ✅ Described your customer and what their car must do
- ✅ Sketched your design before building
- ✅ Built a concept model with a special feature

**You are a car designer now!**
