Design it → **engineer it to perform** → manufacture it.

---

# Propulsion: the balloon engine 🎈

Blow up a balloon. Let it go.

**Why does it fly?**

- Air pushes out **backward**
- The balloon goes **forward**
- That's **thrust** — action and reaction

---

# Today's engine is a balloon 💨

The engineering isn't the balloon.

### It's making that thrust move a car **straight and far**.

---

# Build the balloon car 🛠️

Your team builds:
- A **chassis**
- **Free-spinning** axles
- **Wheels**
- A balloon with a **straw nozzle** taped on

**First milestone:** it moves under its own power.

---

# Classic failures (and they're all fixable) 🔍

- Wheels rubbing the chassis → **friction**
- Nozzle pinched → no thrust
- Car too heavy → goes nowhere

**Ask yourself: where is it losing energy?**

---

# Test Trials — with DATA 📋

Three timed rounds. Every round:

1. **Run** it
2. **Measure** the distance 📏
3. **Record** it on the data sheet
4. Change **exactly one variable**
5. **Predict** the effect before re-running

---

# Crash Safety Challenge 🥚

**New requirement:** your car carries an egg passenger down the
ramp into the wall…

### …and the passenger must SURVIVE.

Engineer restraints and crumple zones. Then crash-test.
Redesign once if your passenger gets hurt.

---

# Real automotive safety 🛡️

### The vehicle can fail.

### The passenger can't.

---
