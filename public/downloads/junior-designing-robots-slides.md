# Robot Anatomy 🦾

Every robot has five part groups:

| Part | Like your... | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Sensors** | eyes & ears 👀 | detect the world |
| **Controller** | brain 🧠 | make decisions |
| **Chassis** | body 🦴 | hold it together |
| **Motors** | muscles 💪 | create movement |
| **Effectors** | hands 🖐️ | do the actual job |

# The Engineering Design Process 🔄

How real engineers work:

**DEFINE** the problem →
**BRAINSTORM** ideas →
**PLAN** your design →
**BUILD** it →
**TEST** it →
**IMPROVE** it → *(and loop back!)*

You'll use ALL these steps today.

---

# Design Sprint: Robots That Help 💗

In teams of 2–3, pick a mission:
- 🛒 Help an elderly neighbor carry groceries
- 🐈 Find a lost pet
- 🌱 Water a garden automatically
- 🗑️ Clean up a park

---

# Step 1–2: Define & Plan (25 min) ✏️

On your planning sheet:
1. Who are you helping? What's their problem exactly?
2. **Sketch your robot** and label:
   - Its sensors
   - Its brain
   - How it moves
   - Its tool
3. List your materials

**Engineers plan BEFORE they build!**

---

# Step 3: Build (30 min) 🔨

Build your model! Rules:
- Every labeled part must be represented (a bottle cap can be a camera!)
- Everyone on the team builds
- It doesn't have to work — it has to **show your design**

---

# Step 4: Test & Improve (15 min) 🧪

A mentor will visit as your **skeptical customer**:

> *"Hmm... what happens when it rains?"*
> *"How does it know where the garden ends?"*

Answer their hard question, then make **ONE improvement** to your design.

---

# Design Review Circle ⭐

Each team: 90-second pitch!
1. The problem
2. The parts
3. The improvement you made

Audience gives **Two Stars and a Wish**:
⭐⭐ two things you liked + 🌠 one suggestion
