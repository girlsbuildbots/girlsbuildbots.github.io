## About this badge

Cadettes explore the psychology of happiness — what research says actually makes people happier — and test science-backed practices on themselves.

:::note[Official requirements]
The step-by-step requirements for the Cadette Science of Happiness badge are
published by Girl Scouts of the USA. Troop leaders access them free in the
[Volunteer Toolkit](https://mygs.girlscouts.org), or purchase the badge and requirement pamphlet
from the [Girl Scout Shop](https://www.girlscoutshop.com/badges-program/all-badges-pins/stem). Free sample activities for many
badges are on the [Girl Scout Activity Zone](https://www.girlscouts.org/en/activities-for-girls.html).

*Always verify workshop activities against the official steps before awarding the badge.*
:::

## Leading the meeting

Run real experiments: mood check-in, then stations from Greater Good in Action (gratitude letters, savoring, acts of kindness), then mood check-out. Graph the before/after data — Cadettes love that the results are THEIR results.

## Best free resources

- [Greater Good in Action — science-tested happiness practices](https://ggia.berkeley.edu)
- [Science Buddies — forensics & science activities](https://www.sciencebuddies.org)

Planning help — mentor ratios, timing, surveys, and a 6-week checklist — is in the
[Resource Guide](/downloads/resource-guide.md).
