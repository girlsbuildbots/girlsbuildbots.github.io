## About this badge

This badge challenges Cadettes to prototype a robot that helps people overcome real obstacles — assistive technology, home care, rescue, accessibility. When she's earned it, a Cadette has run a professional-style design process from user interview to design review.

**Scouts will learn:**

- Assistive robotics: prosthetics, companions, rescue, accessibility
- User-centered design — interviewing before sketching
- Decision matrices: comparing concepts against chosen criteria
- Failure-mode analysis: asking 'what breaks first?'

:::note[Official requirements]
The step-by-step requirements for the Cadette Designing Robots badge are
published by Girl Scouts of the USA. Troop leaders access them free in the
[Volunteer Toolkit](https://mygs.girlscouts.org), or purchase the badge and requirement pamphlet
from the [Girl Scout Shop](https://www.girlscoutshop.com/badges-program/all-badges-pins/stem). Free sample activities for many
badges are on the [Girl Scout Activity Zone](https://www.girlscouts.org/en/activities-for-girls.html).

*Always verify workshop activities against the official steps before awarding the badge.*
:::

## Meeting plan

<p class="meeting-chips">
  <Badge text="⏱ 2.5 hours" variant="tip" size="medium" />
  <Badge text="👥 1 mentor : 5–6 Scouts" variant="tip" size="medium" />
  <Badge text="💻 MakeCode Arcade — free" variant="tip" size="medium" />
</p>

- Assistive robotics case studies (25 min)
- Design Challenge: user interview → two concepts → decision matrix → prototype → failure analysis & iteration (90 min)
- Engineering Design Review before a mentor panel (35 min)

**Materials:** Challenge briefs, engineering notebooks, prototyping supplies (foam board, dowels, syringes + tubing for hydraulics). Optional: LEGO Technic, VEX IQ, micro:bit + servos.

## Completion checklist

By the end of the session, each Scout:

- [ ] Analyzed real assistive robots and their users
- [ ] Documented a problem statement for a specific user
- [ ] Compared multiple concepts using a decision matrix
- [ ] Built a prototype and made one documented iteration
- [ ] Defended the design in a review
