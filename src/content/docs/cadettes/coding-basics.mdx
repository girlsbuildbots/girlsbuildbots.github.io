## About this badge

Cadettes explore how code powers the technology they use daily and write programs using core CS concepts. Using computer science for good is the through-line of the whole series.

:::note[Official requirements]
The step-by-step requirements for the Cadette Coding Basics badge are
published by Girl Scouts of the USA. Troop leaders access them free in the
[Volunteer Toolkit](https://mygs.girlscouts.org), or purchase the badge and requirement pamphlet
from the [Girl Scout Shop](https://www.girlscoutshop.com/badges-program/all-badges-pins/stem). Free sample activities for many
badges are on the [Girl Scout Activity Zone](https://www.girlscouts.org/en/activities-for-girls.html).

*Always verify workshop activities against the official steps before awarding the badge.*
:::

## Leading the meeting

Works beautifully as a MakeCode Arcade session — reuse the Programming Robots deck's Arcade build as your hands-on core, then have leaders map activities to the official steps in the VTK.

## Best free resources

- [MakeCode Arcade — free block coding in the browser](https://arcade.makecode.com)
- [Code.org — free structured courses & Hour of Code](https://code.org)
- [MIT App Inventor — build real Android apps free](https://appinventor.mit.edu)
- [Girls Who Code — free clubs & programs](https://girlswhocode.com)

Planning help — mentor ratios, timing, surveys, and a 6-week checklist — is in the
