## About this badge

This badge is about sharing robot designs publicly and joining the robotics community. When she's earned it, a Cadette has delivered a jargon-free public pitch, handled live Q&A, and left with a concrete next step into robotics.

**Scouts will learn:**

- The robotics ecosystem: FLL, FTC, FRC, and every role on a team
- Translating technical work for a general audience — killing the jargon
- The public pitch: hook, solution, evidence, ask
- Handling Q&A: 'we'd need to test that' is a professional answer

:::note[Official requirements]
The step-by-step requirements for the Cadette Showcasing Robots badge are
published by Girl Scouts of the USA. Troop leaders access them free in the
[Volunteer Toolkit](https://mygs.girlscouts.org), or purchase the badge and requirement pamphlet
from the [Girl Scout Shop](https://www.girlscoutshop.com/badges-program/all-badges-pins/stem). Free sample activities for many
badges are on the [Girl Scout Activity Zone](https://www.girlscouts.org/en/activities-for-girls.html).

*Always verify workshop activities against the official steps before awarding the badge.*
:::

## Meeting plan

<p class="meeting-chips">

- Pathways Into Robotics — ecosystem talk + live FRC teardown demo (25 min)
- Prepare the Public Pitch with two timed rehearsals (50 min)
- Public Showcase with audience Q&A (35 min)
- Next steps: teams, programs, and contacts to keep going (10 min)

**Materials:** Prototypes and notebooks from the Designing badge, presentation tools, the FRC robot, and printed info on local FIRST teams.

By the end of the session, each Scout:

- [ ] Learned about FIRST programs, team roles, and career paths
- [ ] Prepared a jargon-free pitch with a human-centered hook
- [ ] Presented publicly and handled audience questions
- [ ] Identified a next step to continue in robotics
