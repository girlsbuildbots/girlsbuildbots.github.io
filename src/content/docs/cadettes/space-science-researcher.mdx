## About this badge

Cadettes research the universe like astronomers — exploring stars, light, and spectra, and learning how scientists decode information from distant objects.

:::note[Official requirements]
The step-by-step requirements for the Cadette Space Science Researcher badge are
published by Girl Scouts of the USA. Troop leaders access them free in the
[Volunteer Toolkit](https://mygs.girlscouts.org), or purchase the badge and requirement pamphlet
from the [Girl Scout Shop](https://www.girlscoutshop.com/badges-program/all-badges-pins/stem). Free sample activities for many
badges are on the [Girl Scout Activity Zone](https://www.girlscouts.org/en/activities-for-girls.html).

*Always verify workshop activities against the official steps before awarding the badge.*
:::

## Leading the meeting

Diffraction-grating glasses turn every light source into a spectrum lab: compare bulb types, then connect to how astronomers read starlight. End with real telescope imagery and Stellarium sky navigation. Tie-in: space robots collect this data.
