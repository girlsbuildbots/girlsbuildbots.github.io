## Downloads — every badge, every format

Each badge has a **lesson plan** (the mentor's teaching guide) and a **slide
deck** (what you present). Every one comes in three formats:

- **PDF** — print it, or open it anywhere
- **PPTX** — edit in PowerPoint, or upload to Google Drive to convert to Slides
- **MD** — the markdown source; decks are Marp-compatible

The three Robotics badges at each level share one full series curriculum instead
of separate plans. Nine lesson plans — all seven Cadette badges outside Robotics,
plus STEM Career Exploration at every level — are published in Canva instead of
here; their row links straight to the Canva design, and their slide decks are
still downloadable in all three formats.

:::tip[Editing the materials]
The markdown files are the source of truth. Edit one, re-run `npm run decks`,
and the PDF and PowerPoint regenerate under the same filenames — so every link
on this site keeps working.
:::
