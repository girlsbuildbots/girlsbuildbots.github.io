import { Card, CardGrid, LinkCard } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Why this exists" icon="puzzle">
    Robotics outreach events are everywhere — but most aren't structured to let
    Girl Scouts earn their *official* badges. Troop leaders often don't have the
    technical background to run STEM badge work themselves, and robotics teams
    don't know what badge requirements look like. Girls end up doing great STEM
    activities that count for… nothing on their vest.

    Girls Build Bots closes that gap with a **badge library** covering the STEM
    badges at each level, **ready-to-teach curriculum, slide decks, and mentor
    guides** for the Robotics series, and a **pairing hub** where troops and
    teams find each other.
  </Card>

  <Card title="How a workshop works" icon="rocket">
    1. **Troop leaders pick badges and review the official requirements** in the
       free Volunteer Toolkit, then order badges from the council shop.
    2. **The robotics team preps with our materials** — full meeting plans,
       slide decks, mentor guides, and checklists. Mentor training takes about
       an hour.
    3. **Scouts build, program, and present** — capped with a real
       competition-robot demo and a showcase for families. Then leave a review
       on the badge page so the next team teaches it even better.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
