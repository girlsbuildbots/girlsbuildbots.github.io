## About this badge

Juniors explore biomimicry — how engineers borrow nature's best designs — and design a solution to a problem inspired by a plant or animal.

:::note[Official requirements]
The step-by-step requirements for the Junior Design with Nature badge are
published by Girl Scouts of the USA. Troop leaders access them free in the
[Volunteer Toolkit](https://mygs.girlscouts.org), or purchase the badge and requirement pamphlet
from the [Girl Scout Shop](https://www.girlscoutshop.com/badges-program/all-badges-pins/stem). Free sample activities for many
badges are on the [Girl Scout Activity Zone](https://www.girlscouts.org/en/activities-for-girls.html).

*Always verify workshop activities against the official steps before awarding the badge.*
:::

## Leading the meeting

Tell the Velcro story (burrs → hook-and-loop), browse AskNature for inspiration, then teams design a nature-inspired invention. The robotics hook is irresistible: gecko-grip climbing robots, bird-wing drones, snake robots.

## Best free resources

- [AskNature — biomimicry: design inspired by nature](https://asknature.org)
- [PBS Design Squad — engineering design challenges](https://pbskids.org/designsquad)

Planning help — mentor ratios, timing, surveys, and a 6-week checklist — is in the
[Resource Guide](/downloads/resource-guide.md).
