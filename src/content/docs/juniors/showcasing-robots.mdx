## About this badge

This badge is about sharing robot designs with an audience and discovering the wider robotics world. When she's earned it, a Junior has built a real presentation, delivered it science-fair style, and seen where robotics can take her next.

**Scouts will learn:**

- What competitive robotics looks like — and the many roles on a team
- Turning a design into a four-part presentation
- Presenting as a team: everyone speaks, practice twice
- Why the 'what went wrong' story is the best part of any engineering talk

:::note[Official requirements]
The step-by-step requirements for the Junior Showcasing Robots badge are
published by Girl Scouts of the USA. Troop leaders access them free in the
[Volunteer Toolkit](https://mygs.girlscouts.org), or purchase the badge and requirement pamphlet
from the [Girl Scout Shop](https://www.girlscoutshop.com/badges-program/all-badges-pins/stem). Free sample activities for many
badges are on the [Girl Scout Activity Zone](https://www.girlscouts.org/en/activities-for-girls.html).

*Always verify workshop activities against the official steps before awarding the badge.*
:::

<p class="meeting-chips">
  <Badge text="⏱ 2 hours" variant="tip" size="medium" />

- The World of Robotics — team presentation + match video (25 min)
- Build Your Showcase — poster, slides, or demo theater (45 min)
- Showcase Fair with rotating audience + fun superlatives (40 min)
- Reflection and badge confirmation (10 min)

**Materials:** Models and design sheets from the Designing badge, poster boards or a slide template, competition match video, FRC robot if available.

## Completion checklist

By the end of the session, each Scout:

- [ ] Learned about robotics competitions and team roles
- [ ] Created a presentation covering problem, design, and iteration
- [ ] Presented to an audience and answered questions
- [ ] Reflected on what she would build next
