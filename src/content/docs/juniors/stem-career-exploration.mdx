## About this badge

Juniors explore STEM careers across computer science, engineering, health, environment, and design — and connect their own interests and skills to real jobs. This badge pairs perfectly with a robotics team visit — every mentor is a live example of a STEM path in progress.

:::note[Official requirements]
The step-by-step requirements for the Junior STEM Career Exploration badge are
published by Girl Scouts of the USA. Troop leaders access them free in the
[Volunteer Toolkit](https://mygs.girlscouts.org), or purchase the badge and requirement pamphlet
from the [Girl Scout Shop](https://www.girlscoutshop.com/badges-program/all-badges-pins/stem). Free sample activities for many
badges are on the [Girl Scout Activity Zone](https://www.girlscouts.org/en/activities-for-girls.html).

*Always verify workshop activities against the official steps before awarding the badge.*
:::

## Leading the meeting

The strongest session is a career panel: 4–5 team members each give a 2-minute 'my STEM story,' then Scouts rotate through 'career stations' matching team roles — programming, CAD, strategy, media. End with each Scout picking one career to explore and one step to take.
