<CardGrid>
  <Card title="For Girl Scout troops" icon="star">
    **Find a FIRST robotics team near you**

    - [FIRST Team & Event Search](https://www.firstinspires.org/team-event-search) —
      the official locator. Search your ZIP code for FRC and FTC teams; team
      pages list contact info.
    - Ask your local high school — most FRC teams are school-based and reachable
      through the front office or an engineering teacher.
    - Attend a competition (free to watch!) and talk to teams at their pits —
      they love visitors.
    - In metro Atlanta? **Walton Robotics (FRC 2974)** helped build this
      program — use the pairing form below.
  </Card>

  <Card title="For robotics teams" icon="setting">
    **Find Girl Scout troops to serve**

    - Contact your local Girl Scout council's program staff — in metro Atlanta
      that's [Girl Scouts of Greater Atlanta](https://www.girlscoutsatl.org);
      elsewhere, use the
      [council finder](https://www.girlscouts.org/en/footer/find-your-council.html).
    - Councils can post your workshop in their event calendars and reach
      hundreds of troops at once.
    - Service Unit meetings (local troop leader gatherings) are a great place to
      pitch a workshop.
    - Everything you need to teach is free on this site: curriculum, slides,
      mentor guides, and surveys.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
