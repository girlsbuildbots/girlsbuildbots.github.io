## About this badge

**Badge theme:** Learning how computers and networks share information, and why protecting personal information matters online.

### The through-line: being safe and smart online

The Cybersecurity series builds digital street-smarts: understand how information travels (**Basics**), protect it (**Safeguards**), and think critically to solve problems (**Investigator**). For Brownies, keep it concrete and friendly — never scary.

**Vocabulary:** information, private, network, message, safe, personal information.

:::note[Official requirements]
The step-by-step requirements for the Brownie Cybersecurity Basics badge are
published by Girl Scouts of the USA. Troop leaders access them free in the
[Volunteer Toolkit](https://mygs.girlscouts.org), or purchase the badge and requirement pamphlet
from the [Girl Scout Shop](https://www.girlscoutshop.com/badges-program/all-badges-pins/stem). Free sample activities for many
badges are on the [Girl Scout Activity Zone](https://www.girlscouts.org/en/activities-for-girls.html).

*Always verify workshop activities against the official steps before awarding the badge.*
:::

### Activity 1 — Pass the Message (Network Game) (15 min)

Scouts stand in a circle and pass a message ("the robot is blue") hand to hand — that's a **network**, how information travels between computers. Then add a "listener" in the middle who tries to overhear. **Point:** messages travel through many hands, so some information we keep private.

### Activity 2 — Private or Share-Able? (15 min)

Sort cards into two piles: things that are fine to share (favorite animal, your team name) vs. **personal information** we keep private (full name + address, phone number, passwords). Mentors guide the tricky ones. This is the core Basics idea — knowing the difference.

### Activity 3 — Play Interland (35 min)

Scouts play the friendly, age-appropriate Interland game (mentor-led, one realm). It reinforces sharing wisely and being kind online, through play. Mentors circulate and connect what happens in the game back to the sorting activity.

### Activity 4 — My Three Rules (5 min) + Wrap (5 min)

Each Scout names one thing she'll keep private and one thing that's fine to share. Closing circle: thumbs up if you can spot personal information now.

:::tip[Robot moment (optional)]
Share for 60 seconds how the team keeps its robot code and passwords private so no other team can change their robot — being careful with information matters in robotics too.
:::

### Materials

- Devices with **Interland** (Google's *Be Internet Awesome*, beinternetawesome.withgoogle.com) — the "Mindful Mountain" and "Kind Kingdom" realms fit best
- Yarn or a ball to pass, for the network game
- "Private vs. OK-to-share" sorting cards (favorite color = OK; home address = private)

## Completion checklist

By the end of the session, to earn the badge, each Scout can:

- [ ] Explain that a network is how information travels between computers
- [ ] Tell the difference between personal information and things that are OK to share
- [ ] Play through an online-safety activity and name what it taught
- [ ] Name at least one piece of personal information she'll keep private
