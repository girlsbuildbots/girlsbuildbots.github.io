## About this badge

This badge is about sharing robot designs with others. When she's earned it, a Brownie has seen how robots are shared with the world, prepared a short presentation about her own design, and delivered it to an audience.

**Scouts will learn:**

- How engineers share their work — competitions, presentations, websites
- Meeting a real competition robot up close (the highlight!)
- A four-line presentation frame every Brownie can deliver
- Presenting skills: volume, eye contact, holding your work up proudly

:::note[Official requirements]
The step-by-step requirements for the Brownie Showcasing Robots badge are
published by Girl Scouts of the USA. Troop leaders access them free in the
[Volunteer Toolkit](https://mygs.girlscouts.org), or purchase the badge and requirement pamphlet
from the [Girl Scout Shop](https://www.girlscoutshop.com/badges-program/all-badges-pins/stem). Free sample activities for many
badges are on the [Girl Scout Activity Zone](https://www.girlscouts.org/en/activities-for-girls.html).

*Always verify workshop activities against the official steps before awarding the badge.*
:::

## Meeting plan

<p class="meeting-chips">
  <Badge text="⏱ 90 minutes" variant="tip" size="medium" />

- Meet a real robot — FRC demo + Q&A (20 min)
- Prepare the Robot Reveal with a fill-in frame (30 min)
- The Showcase — every Scout presents, everyone claps (30 min)
- Badge ceremony wrap-up (10 min)

**Materials:** Prototypes and design sheets from the Designing badge, a showcase table, and — if at all possible — the team's competition robot.

## Completion checklist

By the end of the session, each Scout:

- [ ] Saw a working robot and asked a question about it
- [ ] Prepared a short presentation about her design
- [ ] Presented to an audience
- [ ] Reflected on one thing she would improve
