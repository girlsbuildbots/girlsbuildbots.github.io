## About this badge

**Badge theme:** Sharing and presenting robot designs — from explaining a robot’s purpose to showcasing its features and impact.

### The through-line: engineering *for girls*

This is the capstone robotics badge: Brownies showcase a robot design by explaining the problem it solves, who it helps, and what makes it useful. The focus is on sharing ideas, celebrating creativity, and building confidence through presentation.

**Vocabulary:** robot, showcase, presentation, audience, design, prototype, engineer, demonstrate, feedback, improve

:::note[Official requirements]
The step-by-step requirements for the Brownie Showcasing Robots badge are
published by Girl Scouts of the USA. Troop leaders access them free in the
[Volunteer Toolkit](https://mygs.girlscouts.org), or purchase the badge and requirement pamphlet
from the [Girl Scout Shop](https://www.girlscoutshop.com/badges-program/all-badges-pins/stem). Free sample activities for many
badges are on the [Girl Scout Activity Zone](https://www.girlscouts.org/en/activities-for-girls.html).

*Always verify workshop activities against the official steps before awarding the badge.*
:::

### Materials

1. Robot prototypes, models, or design sheets from the Designing Robots badge
2. A working robot for a demonstration, preferably an FRC robot
3. Paper, pencils, and markers
4. Robot Reveal worksheet (included in lesson plan)
5. Presentation Practice Checklist
6. Optional: photos or videos of robots

### Activity 1 — Meet a Real Robot (20 min)

1. Ask Scouts where they have seen robots and how people learn about them
2. Explain that engineers share their work through competitions, presentations, websites, and demonstrations
3. Invite a robotics team member to demonstrate a robot and explain what it does
4. Let Scouts ask questions, such as:
  - What does the robot do?
  - Who built it?
  - What was the hardest part to build?
  - How do you show other people what your robot can do?

### Activity 2 — Plan Your Robot Presentation (15 min)

1. Have each Scout choose a robot, prototype, or drawing from the Designing Robots badge
2. Give each Scout the Robot Reveal worksheet
3. Ask Scouts to draw their robot and identify the problem it solves, who it helps, and one special feature
4. Have Scouts complete these four sentences:
  - My robot is called...
  - It helps... by...
  - One special feature is...
  - If I improved it, I would...

### Activity 3 — Practice Presenting (15 min)

1. Teach three presentation skills: speak clearly, look toward the audience, and show your robot or drawing
2. Pair Scouts and have them practice their presentations together
3. Ask partners to share one thing the presenter did well
4. Have each Scout choose one thing to improve and practice again

### Activity 4 — The Showcase (30 min)

1. Set up a presentation area for Scouts to display their robots/creations
2. Invite each Scout to give her short presentation
3. Encourage Scouts to show their designs and explain how they work
4. After each presentation, invite the audience to share something they liked or ask a question
5. Remind Scouts to listen respectfully and celebrate everyone's ideas

### Activity 5 — Reflect and Celebrate (10 min)

Ask Scouts:
  - What did you learn about sharing robot designs?
  - What part of your presentation are you proud of?
  - What would you improve about your robot?
  - What did you learn from another Scout?

Celebrate everyone's effort and thank Scouts for sharing their work.

## Completion checklist

By the end of the session, to earn the badge, each Scout can:

- [ ] Observe a robot and ask a question
- [ ] Prepare a short presentation about her design
- [ ] Present her design to an audience
- [ ] Reflect on what she learned and what she could improve
