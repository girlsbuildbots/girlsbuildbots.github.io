## About this badge

Brownies design and create their own games — inventing goals and rules, making them fair and fun, and improving them through playtesting.

:::note[Official requirements]
The step-by-step requirements for the Brownie Making Games badge are
published by Girl Scouts of the USA. Troop leaders access them free in the
[Volunteer Toolkit](https://mygs.girlscouts.org), or purchase the badge and requirement pamphlet
from the [Girl Scout Shop](https://www.girlscoutshop.com/badges-program/all-badges-pins/stem). Free sample activities for many
badges are on the [Girl Scout Activity Zone](https://www.girlscouts.org/en/activities-for-girls.html).

*Always verify workshop activities against the official steps before awarding the badge.*
:::

## Leading the meeting

Perfect FIRST tie-in: explain that a brand-new robot game is invented every single year! Scout teams design a mini 'robot game' (goals, rules, scoring), then the mentors playtest it and Scouts revise the rules — real game design iteration.

## Best free resources

- [PBS Design Squad — engineering design challenges](https://pbskids.org/designsquad)
- [ScratchJr — tablet coding for early readers](https://www.scratchjr.org)
