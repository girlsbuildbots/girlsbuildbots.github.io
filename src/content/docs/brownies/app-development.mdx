## About this badge

Brownies explore how apps solve problems for people and imagine an app that helps their community. Using computer science for good is the through-line of the whole series.

:::note[Official requirements]
The step-by-step requirements for the Brownie App Development badge are
published by Girl Scouts of the USA. Troop leaders access them free in the
[Volunteer Toolkit](https://mygs.girlscouts.org), or purchase the badge and requirement pamphlet
from the [Girl Scout Shop](https://www.girlscoutshop.com/badges-program/all-badges-pins/stem). Free sample activities for many
badges are on the [Girl Scout Activity Zone](https://www.girlscouts.org/en/activities-for-girls.html).

*Always verify workshop activities against the official steps before awarding the badge.*
:::

## Leading the meeting

Run a paper-prototyping session: Scouts sketch app screens for a problem they care about, then 'click test' each other's paper apps.

## Best free resources

- [Code.org — free structured courses & Hour of Code](https://code.org)
- [Scratch — free coding & game design (MIT)](https://scratch.mit.edu)

Planning help — mentor ratios, timing, surveys, and a 6-week checklist — is in the
[Resource Guide](/downloads/resource-guide.md).
