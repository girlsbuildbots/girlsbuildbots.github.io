## About this badge

Brownies practice the safeguards that keep them safe online — passwords, permission, and what (not) to share.

:::note[Official requirements]
The step-by-step requirements for the Brownie Cybersecurity Safeguards badge are
published by Girl Scouts of the USA. Troop leaders access them free in the
[Volunteer Toolkit](https://mygs.girlscouts.org), or purchase the badge and requirement pamphlet
from the [Girl Scout Shop](https://www.girlscoutshop.com/badges-program/all-badges-pins/stem). Free sample activities for many
badges are on the [Girl Scout Activity Zone](https://www.girlscouts.org/en/activities-for-girls.html).

*Always verify workshop activities against the official steps before awarding the badge.*
:::

## Leading the meeting

Run a password-strength lab (test made-up passwords, never real ones!) and a 'spot the phish' game with mentor-made example messages.

## Best free resources

- [Be Internet Awesome / Interland — Google's online-safety game](https://beinternetawesome.withgoogle.com)
- [CYBER.ORG — free K–12 cybersecurity lessons](https://cyber.org)
- [NOVA Cybersecurity Lab — free defense simulation game](https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/nova/labs/lab/cyber/)

Planning help — mentor ratios, timing, surveys, and a 6-week checklist — is in the
[Resource Guide](/downloads/resource-guide.md).
