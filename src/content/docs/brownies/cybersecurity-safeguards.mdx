## About this badge

**Badge theme:** Practicing the safeguards that keep us safe online — strong passwords, permission, and what (not) to share.

### The through-line: being safe and smart online

The Cybersecurity series builds digital street-smarts: understand how information travels (Basics), **protect it** (Safeguards), and think critically to solve problems (Investigator). Keep it concrete and friendly — never scary.

**Vocabulary:** password, protect, safeguard, secret, strong vs. weak, permission, ask a grown-up.

:::note[Official requirements]
The step-by-step requirements for the Brownie Cybersecurity Safeguards badge are
published by Girl Scouts of the USA. Troop leaders access them free in the
[Volunteer Toolkit](https://mygs.girlscouts.org), or purchase the badge and requirement pamphlet
from the [Girl Scout Shop](https://www.girlscoutshop.com/badges-program/all-badges-pins/stem). Free sample activities for many
badges are on the [Girl Scout Activity Zone](https://www.girlscouts.org/en/activities-for-girls.html).

*Always verify workshop activities against the official steps before awarding the badge.*
:::

### Activity 1 — The Secret Latch (Passwords) (15 min)

A password is like the latch on a treasure box — it keeps your things safe. Show the box: a weak latch (anyone can open it) vs. a strong one. Sort **make-believe** password cards into strong (long, mixed, silly-but-memorable) vs. weak ("1234," "password"). **Never use anyone's real passwords** — invented examples only.

### Activity 2 — Play Interland: Tower of Treasure (30 min)

Scouts play the password-themed realm, building strong "keys" through the game. Mentors connect it back to the treasure-box idea: long and unexpected is strong.

### Activity 3 — Ask a Grown-Up (15 min)

Scenario cards: a pop-up says "you won a prize!", a game asks for your full name, someone you don't know sends a message. For each, Scouts vote: safe, or **stop and ask a trusted grown-up?** The safeguard here is knowing when to pause and ask.

### Activity 4 — My Safeguards (5 min) + Wrap (5 min)

Each Scout names one way she'll keep her information safe (a strong password, asking a grown-up). Thumbs up if you know what makes a password strong.

:::tip[Robot moment (optional)]
Share for 60 seconds how the team uses strong passwords to protect its robot code and its accounts — the same safeguards, for a robot.
:::

### Materials

- A "treasure box" with a simple lock/latch (a prop for the password idea)
- **Make-believe** password cards to sort strong vs. weak (never real passwords)
- Devices with **Interland** (beinternetawesome.withgoogle.com) — the "Tower of Treasure" realm is about strong passwords
- "Ask a grown-up?" scenario cards (a pop-up appears, a stranger messages you, a game asks for your name)

## Completion checklist

By the end of the session, to earn the badge, each Scout can:

- [ ] Explain what a password does and what makes one strong vs. weak
- [ ] Build or identify a strong (make-believe) password
- [ ] Name a situation where she should stop and ask a trusted grown-up
- [ ] Name one safeguard she'll use to protect her information
