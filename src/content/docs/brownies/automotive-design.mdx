## About this badge

Brownies think like car designers: who is the vehicle for, what does it need to do, and what should it look like? They sketch and model a vehicle of their own design.

:::note[Official requirements]
The step-by-step requirements for the Brownie Automotive Design badge are
published by Girl Scouts of the USA. Troop leaders access them free in the
[Volunteer Toolkit](https://mygs.girlscouts.org), or purchase the badge and requirement pamphlet
from the [Girl Scout Shop](https://www.girlscoutshop.com/badges-program/all-badges-pins/stem). Free sample activities for many
badges are on the [Girl Scout Activity Zone](https://www.girlscouts.org/en/activities-for-girls.html).

*Always verify workshop activities against the official steps before awarding the badge.*
:::

## Leading the meeting

Give each team a 'customer card' (a farmer, a delivery driver, a family with a dog) and have them sketch, then build a cardboard concept model for that customer. Open with a show-and-tell of how your team designs its drivetrain and chassis each season.
