# Sense → Think → Act 🧠

Every robot works the same way:

| SENSE 👀 | THINK 🧠 | ACT 🦾 |
|---|---|---|
| "I see a wall!" | "I should turn!" | *turns around* |
| "It's dark!" | "Lights needed!" | *lights on* |

**Let's play it!** Your mentor calls out what the robot senses — YOU act out what it does!

---

# Engineers design FIRST ✏️

Before building, engineers answer three questions:

1. What does my robot **SENSE**?
2. What does my robot **THINK** about?
3. What does my robot **DO**?

---

# Your turn: Build a Robot Prototype! 🎨

1. Pick a problem: messy room? thirsty plants? heavy backpack?
2. Fill out your design sheet:
   - My robot's **name**
   - My robot's **job**
   - What it **senses**
   - What it **does**
3. Build it with craft supplies!

A **prototype** is a first model — it doesn't have to really work!
