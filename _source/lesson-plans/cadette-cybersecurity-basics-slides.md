# Knowledge over fear

Today you get the honest, technical version:

- How the internet **actually** moves your data

### Someone who understands the system protects herself better than someone who's just been warned about it.

---

# The Packet Game 📨

Real messages don't travel whole.

They're split into **packets** that hop router to router
and reassemble at the destination.

1. Split your message across **three numbered cards**
2. Hand them to different **routers**
3. They arrive out of order — one gets delayed
4. The receiver **reassembles by number**

---

# Round 2: the snooping router 👀

An unprotected card gets **read aloud**.

**Debrief:** your data crosses machines you don't control.

### Which is exactly why encryption exists.

---

# Encryption, for real 🔑

Encrypt with the cipher wheel. Send it through the snooper.

**The snooper now gets gibberish.**

---

# From cipher wheel to HTTPS 🔒

Modern encryption is this same concept —
with math too hard to break.

**HTTPS** — the padlock in your browser — means
it's happening automatically.

### New rule of thumb: no padlock, no passwords.

---

# Where does your data go? 🗺️

For each scenario card, map it out:

- What **data** is generated?
- **Who** receives it?
- Where might it **travel from there**? *(the app's server, advertisers, "free" quiz companies)*

---

# Metadata 📊

Even **without the content**, the *when / where / who*
of your activity tells a story.

### Data is the price of "free."

You should know what you're paying.
