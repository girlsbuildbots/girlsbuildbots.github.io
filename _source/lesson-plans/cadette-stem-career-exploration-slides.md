# Career exploration gets practical

You're close enough to high school that the real questions are:

- What do STEM jobs **actually involve**, day to day?
- What's the **pathway** — classes, clubs, degrees?
- What can you do **this year** that counts?

### Our honest answer: we're on that pathway right now. Here's what it looks like.

---

# Myth vs. Day-in-the-Life 🎴

Read your career card. Report the **surprise**:

### What does this job actually involve — vs. the TV version?

- Software engineers spend more time **reading** code than writing it
- Scientists spend a *lot* of time **writing**

---

# Choose the Tuesdays, not the highlight reel 📅

Every career looks good in a montage.

### Choosing one means knowing what its ordinary Tuesday feels like.

---

# The Panel — Cadette Edition 🎤

Three minutes each, with the practical layer:

- What **classes** we chose, and why
- How we **joined** the team
- What we're aiming for **after high school**
- One **honest struggle**

### Then ask us hard questions. You will — we're ready.

---

# Informational Interviews 🎙️

The badge's core skill, done for real.

In pairs, **8 minutes** with a team member:

1. What does your role involve?
2. What surprised you?
3. What would you tell someone my age?
4. What's the next step on your path?

**Take notes. Then rotate.**

---

# Why this skill matters 💼

Interviewing a person about their path is a career skill
you'll use **for decades**.

Not a workshop exercise. The actual thing.
