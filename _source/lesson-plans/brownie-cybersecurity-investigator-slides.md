# Cybersecurity Investigator 🕵️‍♀️
## Brownie Cybersecurity Badge — Girls Build Bots
### Presented by your robotics mentors!

---

# Today you're a DETECTIVE 🔍

Cyber investigators are detectives.

They **look closely** and **ask questions** before they trust something.

---

# Warm up: spot the difference 👀

Two pictures. One is real. One is a fake.

**Can you find what's different?**

Detectives notice the small details.

---

# Real or Fake? 🃏

We'll show you a message. You hunt for **clues**:

- Too-good-to-be-true prizes? 🎁
- Lots of exclamation marks!!! ❗
- Spelling mistakes? 🔤
- Asking for private information? 🔒

Then vote: **real or fake** — and tell us your clue!

---

# The detective rule 📜

### Don't trust automatically.

### Investigate first.

---

# Crack the Case 🗂️

**The mystery:** someone got a message saying they won a prize.
Should they click it?

In your team, examine your clue cards:
1. The message 📨
2. The sender 👤
3. The link 🔗

**Your verdict: safe, or a trick?** And what clue gave it away?

---

# Robot moment 🤖

When our robot misbehaves, we **investigate**.

We look for clues in the code to find the real problem —
just like a cyber detective.

---

# The Investigator Oath ✋

Finish the sentence:

### "Before I trust something online, I will ___."

- Look for clues 🔍
- Ask a grown-up 🙋
- Check if it's too good to be true 🎁

---
