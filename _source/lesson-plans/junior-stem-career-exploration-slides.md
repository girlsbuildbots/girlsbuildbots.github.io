# Surprise 😮

| Job | The STEM inside |
|---|---|
| Video-game music composer 🎵 | audio technology |
| Sneaker designer 👟 | materials engineering |
| Zookeeper 🦁 | animal science + data |

**Almost every field has a STEM path into it.**

### So your interests already count.

# We started where you are 💫

Nobody handed us a robot.

Every person up here was in fourth grade once.

---

# Career Stations 🔄

Rotate through four stations. **Hands on** at each one:

- 💻 **Programming** → software engineer, roboticist
- 🔧 **Build / CAD** → mechanical engineer, product designer
- 📊 **Strategy / data** → data analyst, engineer-manager
- 📣 **Media / outreach** → technical writer, science communicator

---

# At every station, jot this down 📝

1. **Did I like this?**
2. **What skill did it use?**

Two minutes of *doing* beats ten minutes of listening.
