# We're not just coding today 💚

**Question for you:** who could you help with a computer?

---

# What is an ALGORITHM? 📋

An **algorithm** is just a **list of steps in the right order**.

You already use them every day:
- Brushing your teeth 🪥
- Planting a seed 🌱
- Making a snack 🥣

---

# Game Time: The Algorithm Snack 🥣

Your mentor is now a ROBOT. You give the steps!

- "Make it good" → *the robot FREEZES* ❄️
- Milk before the cup → *a happy mess!* 💦

Computers follow your steps **exactly**.

**So the order matters.**

---

# Unplugged: Algorithm Cards 🃏

With a partner:

1. Put your picture cards in the **right order**
2. Trade cards with your partner
3. **Act out** their algorithm exactly
4. A step missing or out of order? That's a **BUG**! 🐛

---

# Bugs are GOOD! 🐛

When a program doesn't work, that's called a **bug**.

Finding a bug means you're doing **real programming**.

Fixing it is called **debugging**.

---

# Now let's code on a screen! 💻

Follow your mentor, one block at a time:

1. Make a character — that's called a **sprite** ✨
2. Make it **move** across the screen
3. Make it **say hello** or play a sound 🔊

Finished early? Change your character and add a second action!
