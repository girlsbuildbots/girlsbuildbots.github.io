# You can protect yourself 💪

Last time: how information travels.

Today: the **practical protections** you'll actually use.

*The message is "you can protect yourself" — not "the internet is scary."*

---

# Rule #1, out loud 📢

### Every password today is **made up**.

Never write down or share a **real** password.

Not with your friends. Not with a grown-up. **Not with us.**

# The rules you just discovered 🔑

- **Longer beats clever** 📏
- **Mixed characters** help
- A **passphrase** — three random words — is strong *and* memorable

### `PurpleRobotTaco!`

**Your turn:** invent your own passphrase with the method.

---

# The second lock 🔒🔒

Some accounts send a code to a grown-up's phone.

That's called **two-factor**.

### Two locks beat one.

---

# Spot the Phish 🎣

Here's your "inbox." For each message, hunt for **red flags**:

- ⏰ Urgent pressure — "act NOW!"
- 🎁 Prizes for nothing
- 🔤 Misspelled sender addresses
- 🔗 Links that don't match their label
- 🔑 Requests for personal info or passwords

---

# The rule of thumb 👍

### Real companies don't ask for your password in a message.

Unsure? **Don't click. Ask a trusted adult.**

---

# Footprints in the Sand 👣

For each scenario card, ask three questions:

1. **Who can see this?**
2. **How long does it last?**
3. **Would you want it visible in five years?**

Things shared online can **persist and travel**.

**Safeguard:** think before you post, and set privacy settings with a grown-up.

---

# Robot moment 🤖

Our team protects its accounts and code with strong passphrases,
and we **never share logins**.

Losing control of the robot's code would be a very bad day.
