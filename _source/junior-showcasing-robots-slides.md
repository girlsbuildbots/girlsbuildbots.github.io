# Showcasing Robots 🌟
## Junior Robotics Badge — Girls Build Bots
### Presented by your robotics mentors!

---

# Welcome to OUR world 🤖

We're a high school **FIRST Robotics** team!

- We get 6–8 weeks to build a robot the size of a washing machine
- Then we compete against teams from around the world 🌍
- **Watch this competition match video...** 🎬

---

# There's a place for EVERYONE 🧩

Robotics teams need:
- 👩‍💻 **Programmers** — write the robot's brain
- 🔧 **Builders** — design and machine the parts
- 📊 **Strategists** — plan how to win matches
- 📣 **Outreach** — teach the community (that's why we're here!)
- 💼 **Business** — sponsorships and budgets

**You don't have to love ALL of it to belong in robotics.**

---

# Now it's YOUR turn to showcase 🎤

Turn your robot design into a presentation:

1. **The problem** we chose and why it matters
2. **Our robot** — parts and how it works
3. **What went wrong** and how we fixed it
4. **What we'd build next**

Pick your style: poster 🖼️, slides 💻, or live demo theater 🎭

---

# Presentation power tips 💪

- **Everyone speaks** — divide the four parts among your team
- Practice **twice**, out loud, standing up
- Slow down — nervous people talk fast
- The "what went wrong" part is the BEST part — engineers love failure stories!

---

# Showcase Fair! 🎪

Science-fair style:
- Your team at your station
- Visitors rotate through and ask questions
- Answer with confidence — **you're the expert on YOUR design**

Awards incoming... 🏆
*Most Creative Sensor... Best Debugging Story... and more!*
